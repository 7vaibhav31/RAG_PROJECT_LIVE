--------------------------------------------------------------------
OVERVIEW OF ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to the simulation of human intelligence
in machines that are programmed to think like humans and mimic their actions.
The term may also be applied to any machine that exhibits traits associated
with a human mind such as learning and problem-solving.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT IS MACHINE LEARNING?
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Machine Learning (ML) is a subfield of artificial intelligence. It focuses
on developing algorithms and statistical models that enable computers to learn
and make predictions or decisions without being explicitly programmed to do so.
Instead of following static instructions, ML systems use training data to build
mathematical models and improve their performance over time.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
KEY CATEGORIES OF MACHINE LEARNING
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Supervised Learning:
   The algorithm is trained on labeled training data, which means that each
   training example is paired with its correct output label. Examples include
   classification (e.g. email spam detection) and regression tasks.

2. Unsupervised Learning:
   The algorithm is given unlabeled data and must find patterns, groupings,
   or structures within the data on its own. A common example is clustering
   (e.g. grouping customers by purchase behavior).

3. Reinforcement Learning:
   The algorithm learns by interacting with an environment, receiving feedback
   in the form of rewards or penalties, and adjusting its actions to maximize
   the cumulative reward.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
DIFFERENCE BETWEEN DEEP LEARNING AND MACHINE LEARNING
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Deep Learning is a specialized subset of Machine Learning that is based on
artificial neural networks with multiple layers (hence "deep"). While traditional
Machine Learning often requires human engineers to hand-craft feature extraction,
Deep Learning algorithms can automatically learn hierarchical feature representations
directly from raw data (such as pixels of an image or text characters).
